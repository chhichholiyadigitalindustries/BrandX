# BrandX 🚀

> **All-in-One Business Super App for Indian Vyaparis, Retailers & MSMEs**

BrandX empowers small business owners, shopkeepers, and creators to manage **GST Tax Invoices & Thermal POS Receipts**, **Customer Khata (Udhar-Bahi Ledger)**, **Digital Dukaan (1-Page Bio-Link Website)**, **NFC Digital Visiting Cards**, **365-Day Daily Status & Festive Posters**, **Printable UPI QR Standees**, and **Voice-Enabled Biz AI Copilot**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

1. **📒 Digital Khata & Udhar-Bahi Ledger**
   - Track customer credit (Udhar 🔴) vs debit (Jama 🟢) with transaction history.
   - **1-Click WhatsApp Payment Reminder (Taqada):** Auto-generated polite payment reminder message with shop UPI ID and instant pay link.
   - One-click account settlement and balance clearing.
   - Customer search, filtering, and calling integration.

2. **📄 GST & POS Billing Studio**
   - **Multi-Document Modes:** Tax Invoice, Quotation / Estimate (Kaccha Bill), Delivery Challan, and Proforma Invoice.
   - **Item Master / Quick-Add Catalog:** Fast auto-fill from pre-saved product and service lists.
   - **Dual Print Engine:** Standard A4 Executive GST Invoice with authorized seal & 80mm/58mm Thermal POS Receipt for Bluetooth counter printers.
   - Integrated dynamic UPI payment QR code on invoices for instant settlement.
   - 1-Click WhatsApp invoice sharing.

3. **🌐 Digital Dukaan & NFC Smart Visiting Card**
   - **Shareable Online Storefront (Bio-Link):** Mobile website preview with catalog items, tap-to-call, WhatsApp order buttons, Google Maps location, and instant UPI Pay box.
   - **NFC Digital Visiting Card Studio:** Printable double-sided smart cards with QR codes and 1-tap **Download vCard (.vcf)** contact saving.

4. **🗓️ Aaj Ka Status & 365-Day Daily Calendar**
   - Daily morning greetings, suvichar in Hindi/English, and upcoming festival banners.
   - 1-Click **"Brand My Shop"** auto-stamps business logo, phone number, address, and verified badge.
   - Direct 1-Click sharing to WhatsApp Status (9:16).

5. **🎨 Marketing Poster Maker & Creative Canvas**
   - Professional templates for Festivals (Diwali, Eid, Holi), Flash Sales, Weddings, Cafes, Salons & more.
   - Aspect ratio switcher: 1:1 Square (Feed), 9:16 Story/Reel, 16:9 Banner, and 4:5 Portrait.
   - **Trust Badges & Stickers Library:** "100% Pure Veg 🌿", "Made in India 🇮🇳", "Free Delivery 🚚", "COD Available 💵", "Flat 50% OFF 🔥".
   - Regional typography support (Rozha One, Poppins, Inter, Mukta Devanagari).

6. **💳 UPI QR & Payment Standee Studio**
   - Countertop QR standee designer with support for UPI, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM.
   - Google Review QR generator to collect 5-star customer ratings.
   - High-resolution printable standee formats (A4, Table Tent, Acrylic Standee).

7. **🤖 Biz AI Copilot & Voice Assistant**
   - AI marketing assistant designed specifically for Indian business contexts.
   - **Voice Prompts:** Speak naturally in Hindi, Hinglish, or English to create captions and bill prompts.
   - **Google Review AI Auto-Reply:** Generate warm 5-star gratitude responses or polite resolution replies.
   - **WhatsApp Broadcast Campaign Maker:** Craft high-converting festive promotional broadcast messages.

8. **📱 Mobile-First PWA & Google Play Store Ready**
   - Offline-capable Progressive Web App with service worker caching.
   - Persistent `localStorage` sync for khata, catalog, invoices, and business profiles.
   - Trusted Web Activity (TWA) and Android App Bundle (.aab) packaging support.
   - Comprehensive Google Play Data Safety and Privacy Policy integration.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript, Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS, Material Symbols & Lucide Icons
- **Animations:** Motion (Framer Motion)
- **PWA:** Vite Plugin PWA, Workbox Offline Caching
- **AI Integration:** Google Gemini API (`@google/genai`)

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- Node.js (v18 or higher)
- npm or bun

### 1. Installation

```bash
npm install
```

### 2. Run Development Server

```bash
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

### 3. Build for Production

```bash
npm run build
```
